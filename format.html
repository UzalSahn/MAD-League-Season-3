The Draft

We'll all start with [points_num] points. It'll be snake order drafting again; with players who finished lower last season getting precedence over their order. Every roster will need [team_size_min] to [team_size_max] Pokes, staying within the budget — the site won't let you spend down to a point where you can't afford to fill the rest — you always have to leave at least 1 point per remaining slot.

You can build a personal draft queue ahead of time using the star button next them on the Draft Pool (yes I know it's unaligned deal with it). If your clock runs out (5 min time limit so should be fine), auto-pick works through your queue top to bottom instead of just grabbing nothing. If you don't have a queue, it'll take the next highest pick you can afford.

02

Free Agency & Waivers

Anything not drafted is a free agent, claimable for points equal to its tier. Because rosters can never drop below [team_size_min], dropping a Pokémon on its own sounded too annoying to implement. Claiming another is what triggers a drop instead: if your roster's already full, claiming opens a picker asking which Pokémon to send back and both changes land together in the same transaction, so you're never left short a Pokémon. Need more than one for the numbers to work? You can bundle multiple claims and multiple drops together, up to the transaction limit, as long as the count you claim and the count you drop line up exactly. A Pokémon already committed to a pending trade won't show up as an option to drop either — the two systems check each other.

A mon you drop like this sits on waivers for a set number of days, where anyone can place a claim on it. If more than one team wants it, I set rotating priority — whoever's used their "turn" least goes to the front of the line (will be shown on the league table). There'll also be a free agency deadline week — like before. The site'll give a heads-up the week before it hits. Waivers are a new feature that might end up being cancer, but I think it keeps it more fair than first come first serve like before.

This season: each team gets [num_trans_seas] free agency transaction for the whole season, and that transaction can add up to [trade_budget_mons] mons (dropping enough to match). The free agency deadline is Week [free_agency_deadline_week] — so nae claims after that.

03

Trades

The "roster must stay within the league's roster size" rule applies to trades too. The site supports trade requests to anyone but locks the team size to the defined limits, so was really buggy with uneven swaps. Trades therefore always have to be even: offer 1, get 1 back; offer 2, get 2 back. There's no way to throw in an extra drop to balance things out, since that would leave someone short a mon. Geez a break!

When someone gets a request, they'll get notified and can accept, decline, or let it sit ( >:D ). I've set it so trades can only be proposed or accepted Monday to Friday. This at least gives a couple of days for the rest of the league to notice what happened (before we move on with fixtures). Might also set a trade deadline week but trades have been fairly few and far between in the last seasons.

This season: each team gets 2 trades for the whole season, and each trade can move up to 2 Pokémon per side. The trade deadline is Week 6 — no proposing or accepting trades after that.

04

Weekly Matches

The site calculates a random round-robin match schedule. The commissioner announces one week of matchups at a time and decides when the next week begins; future matchups stay unavailable until announced. Each week records when it was announced and when its final result was entered. Week-based deadlines advance when a new week is announced. Any claim or accepted trade made mid-week still doesn't touch your roster until the following Monday — keeps it fair ken haye.

Anyone can input match results with a super simple dead vs. alive interface. The winner can also tag their MVP Pokémon for the match. Once a result's locked in, I (Austin) can still change it, but it'll be locked for everyone else — message me any boo-boos. Click either team's name on a match card to see their full roster and recent transaction history at a glance. There's also a funky (but largely useless) star-wars text scroll thing. Someone please use it at least once? I'll know.

05

Standings & Playoffs

Standings track W, L, Win %, KO Differential, Waiver Priority (see Trades), and a new Streak tracker. Or in Charlie's case a Streak tracker. The regular season is round robin as usual — abdy plays abdy. Ties are broken in a new simpler order (favouring who beat who in the round robin): Wins → KO Differential → Head-to-Head Record → Losses.

Before the postseason, the Playoffs tab will show a live projection of the bracket based on current standings (the mental gymnastics last season about killed me). Once the regular season ends, I'll lock in the seeding — best record faces the worst qualifying record, working inward. With an odd number of playoff teams, the top seed gets a first-round bye; the next round is reseeded so the top remaining seed faces the lowest remaining seed. Players who make it enter results the same way they would in a regular match, and winners automatically advance to the next round. When the championship match locks in, the season is marked complete — a champion banner goes up, free agency and trading close for good, and rosters freeze as final, but it'll all remain browseable which is nice.

06

Rosters, Roles & Commissioner Tools

You all now manage your own team — logo, team name, and first name are all editable from the Team page, and are required before the draft can start. No more asking me to change stuff! If you want to build your logo based on who you draft, just upload a placeholder image and change it afterwards. The pool is also much easier to browse than the spreadsheet: every Pokémon in the Pool, Draft Room, and Free Agency also shows its abilities, and you can search by ability directly which is handy if you're hunting for Intimidater or weather setter etc.

A "Needs Your Attention" panel on the League page also flags anything waiting specifically on you — a trade offer, a match result to enter. Should be easy enough.
